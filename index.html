Gaming Facts Shorts Uploader

A personal tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel Kromossomom-debug to publish short videos about video game history and trivia.

What it does

  • Creates short vertical videos with gaming facts.
  • Uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API, three times a day.

The app is not offered to the public and only accesses the owner's own channel.
