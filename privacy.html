Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026 (TikTok added)

Gaming Facts Shorts Uploader is a personal tool run by the owner of the YouTube channel Kromossomom-debug. It is not offered to other users. It publishes the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel and TikTok account.

Data the app uses

  • With the owner's permission, it uses Google OAuth to access the owner's own YouTube account, with two scopes: uploading videos (youtube.upload) and reading basic channel information (youtube.readonly).
  • With the owner's permission, it uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API on the owner's own TikTok account, with two scopes: basic profile information (user.info.basic) to show which account is connected, and posting videos (video.publish).
  • The OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, sell or share personal data.
  • It does not access any account other than the owner's.
  • It does not use Google user data for advertising or to train AI models.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Google access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. TikTok access can be revoked in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions.
